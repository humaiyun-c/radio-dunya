Radio Dunya

A little window into the world.

Explore live radio with no ads added by this app. Commercials inside a station’s broadcast will still play.

Stations come from Radio Browser, a community directory. This globe includes stations with secure streams your browser can play. Extra coverage across Africa and Asia includes stations without submitted coordinates, placed at an approximate city or capital. When several stations share a dot, tap it to choose from the list. When directory coordinates conflict with a station’s country, a clear city name or the country’s capital supplies an approximate pin. These are marked “Approximate location.” If no fallback is available, the original pin stays. Directory locations can still be incorrect. Some stations may be offline or unavailable in your region.

The Talk genre is a separate collection selected for news, interviews, debate and spoken culture, checked against broadcaster information. It includes speech stations missing from the general map. Occasional music, jingles and station commercials can still play.

Your favorites and recent stations stay in this browser. Nothing plays until you choose a station.

Map: Natural Earth. Location names: GeoNames. Made for listening.
